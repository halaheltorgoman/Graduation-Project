# PCsmith – Smart PC Builder & Community Platform

PCsmith is a modern web application that helps users **build, customize, and share PC builds** with ease.  
Whether you’re a **gamer, content creator, developer, or office worker**, PCsmith guides you step-by-step to assemble a compatible system tailored to your needs.  
Powered by the **MERN stack** with AI integration, PCsmith combines **real-time compatibility checks, guided builds, and community sharing** into one seamless platform.  

---

## Features

- **User Management System** – Sign up, log in, and manage your profile.  
- **Guided PC Builder** – Step-by-step component selection with compatibility checks.  
- **Component Browser** – Search, filter, and favorite PC parts.  
- **AI Chat Assistant** – Chat with an AI to:  
  - Get personalized PC build suggestions  
  - Ask questions about compatibility  
  - Receive alternative part recommendations  
  - Let the AI assemble a build for you based on budget or use-case  
- **Save Builds** – Store your builds for future reference.  
- **Community Feed** – Share builds, explore others, comment, and rate.  
- **Guides Page** – Admin-provided categorized builds (gaming, office, dev, content creation) that users can customize.  

---

##Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: React, TailwindCSS  
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express.js  
- **Database**: MongoDB  
- **AI Integration**: Ollama  
- **Other Tools**: Web scraping for component data, JWT for authentication
